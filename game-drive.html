The Reserves

Majete and Liwonde

Majete Game Reserve and Liwonde National Park are two of the places where our game drives take place. Our driver guides know the tracks and the times of day when animals are most active.

On Safari

What You Can Expect

  • Morning and late-afternoon drives, when wildlife is most active
  • An experienced driver guide who knows the routes
  • Combine with a walking safari or a boat safari on the Shire River at Liwonde
  • Sightings depend on the animals and the season, so none can be promised
