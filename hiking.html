The Mountains

Two Mountains, Two Experiences

Mulanje Mountain rises above tea estates and is known for its trails, forest and views from the high plateau. Michiru Mountain offers its own walks and summit scenery.

Our guides plan each walk around your fitness, group size and the weather on the day.

Before You Go

What to Expect

  • Walks are led by an experienced guide
  • Tell us your fitness level and experience so we can suggest a suitable route
  • Bring suitable footwear, layers and water, and ask us for a full packing list when you enquire
  • Conditions change with weather, so routes are confirmed on the day
