Our Story

How Moyenda Began

The Beginning of Our Journey · January 2024

Every great journey begins with a passion.

Moyenda Tour Guide was born from a passion for exploration, adventure and a desire to share the extraordinary beauty of Malawi with the world.

Established in January 2024 by Aaron Moyenda, a professional and experienced tour guide, the company began with a simple but powerful vision: to help people discover, explore and experience Malawi's natural wonders, wildlife, landscapes and rich cultural heritage — the Warm Heart of Africa.

What started as a passion for tourism grew into a commitment to creating memorable travel experiences. We believe every destination has a story to tell, every adventure creates a memory and every journey brings an opportunity to discover something extraordinary.

Today, we connect travellers with the beauty of Malawi through wildlife encounters, mountain adventures, nature exploration and personalised travel experiences. Our journey is driven by love for adventure, appreciation for our country's natural treasures and a commitment to making every visitor's experience special.

Our promise

We invite you to become part of our journey, experience the beauty of our homeland and create memories that last a lifetime.
